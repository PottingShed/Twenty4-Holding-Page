ABOUT THIS POLICY

This Cookies Policy explains how the Twenty4 website uses cookies and similar technologies. It should be read alongside our Privacy Notice.

WHAT ARE COOKIES?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work, or work more efficiently, and to provide information to the owners of the site.

HOW WE USE COOKIES

This website does not use advertising, analytics or tracking cookies, and we do not use cookies to collect personal data about you.

Our website host may set strictly necessary cookies that are required for the secure and reliable delivery of the site. These cookies do not identify you personally and cannot be switched off in our systems.

MANAGING COOKIES

Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings, including blocking or deleting them. Blocking strictly necessary cookies may affect how the website works. To find out more about cookies, including how to see what cookies have been set, visit www.allaboutcookies.org.

CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

We may update this Cookies Policy from time to time, for example if we introduce new features to our website. Any changes will be posted on this page.
